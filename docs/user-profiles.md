# Target user profiles

SAYCEP is designed around two user profiles:

## 1. Enrolled student

Already enrolled in a qualification (degree, diploma, etc.) and needs help planning what comes next — career pathway, CV, skills gaps relative to their intended role, matching opportunities, interview prep.

## 2. Unqualified youth needing direction

Not currently enrolled or qualified, and needs direction — including trade/artisan paths, not just university/degree routes. This profile likely leans more heavily on the Career Pathway Builder and the TVET/SETA dataset to surface non-degree options.

*(Nothing further drafted yet on how the product experience diverges between these two — e.g. different onboarding flows, different weighting in the matching engine.)*
