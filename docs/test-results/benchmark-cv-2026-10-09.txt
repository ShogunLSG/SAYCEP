SAYCEP — CV scanner benchmark, re-run 9 October 2026 after the plural-folding fix (ADR-0018).
Output of backend/tests/benchmark_cv.py on the shipped code. Same data, split and settings
as 5 October (benchmark-cv-2026-10-05.txt); only the software/tool matching changed.

What changed: "-es" is dropped only after s, x, z, ch, sh (so "Canes" folds to "cane", not
"can"), and a one-word tool name needs 4+ letters ("I saw" no longer names Saws).
