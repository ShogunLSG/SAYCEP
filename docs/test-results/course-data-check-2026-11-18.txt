  Reading Comprehension -> a course on supporting children with reading difficulties
  Monitoring -> cyber-security incident response
  Active Listening -> an English-language course (academic speaking and listening)
  Active Learning, Learning Strategies -> "Learning How to Learn", but only its Spanish edition

Must-have slots with a RELEVANT Coursera course: about 29% (453 of 1,545), not 62%.
The hands-on skills (Operations Monitoring, Operation and Control, Repairing, Troubleshooting,
Equipment Maintenance; 19% of must-have slots) have NO relevant Coursera course. The CC0 Udemy set
is finance only; its few hands-on hits are business courses (maintenance management) or wrong
("credit repair").

CONCLUSION: the licensed Kaggle course data covers office and soft skills, not the hands-on skills
most school-leaver jobs depend on, and keyword matching needs a hand-curated list to be trusted.
Not worth building as an automatic "how to learn it". The source that would cover hands-on skills
is South African training data (TVET courses, SETA learnerships): not on Kaggle, so it needs the
lecturer's approval, as limitation 2 in the report already says.
