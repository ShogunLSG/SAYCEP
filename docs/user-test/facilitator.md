# Facilitator's notes

## Order, per participant

1. Open a **fresh private window** at `http://127.0.0.1:8000`. Presenter mode
   off.
2. Consent (`consent.md`). No consent, no test.
3. Hand over the task sheet's top half. Start the timer when they first touch
   the screen.
4. On the welcome screen, if they reach the optional first name, say
   **"please leave that blank"**, so nothing identifying is stored. That's
   the one thing you tell them.
5. **Watch and count. Don't help.** Note the time, the screens and anything
   that goes wrong (below).
6. Stop the timer when they say they're done, or when the Pathway is on screen
   and they've stopped, or at 10 minutes.
7. The three questions, then the SUS.
8. Fill in their row in `results.md` straight away, while you remember it.

## What you say

- At the start: "Please think aloud as you go. Say what you're looking at and
  what you expect to happen."
- If they go quiet: "What are you thinking now?"
- If they ask you how to do something: "What would you try?" If they're
  still stuck after about 30 seconds, help them, and **record it as an
  assist**.

## What you don't say

- Don't explain the screens, the words "Pathway" or "gap", or what the score
  means.
- Don't say "nearly there" or "that's right". Both are hints.

## What you count

| Measure | How |
|---|---|
| **Reached the Pathway** | yes if the Pathway screen (the ordered steps) shows without an assist |
| **Screens** | count from **"Where are you starting from?"**, because NFR-02 measures from profile creation. Count each screen they land on, and count it again if they go back and forward. The welcome screen before it isn't counted; note separately if it held them up. The design's minimum is 5 |
| **Time** | from the first touch until they say "done" or reach the Pathway |
| **Assists** | each time you helped. Any assist means NFR-02 isn't met for that person |
| **Problems** | where, and what happened, in a few words ("Skills screen: didn't know the chips were tappable") |
| **Plan understood** | from question 1: yes, partly or no. Did they describe a target job and steps towards it? |

## Scoring the SUS

For odd items, subtract 1 from the answer. For even items, subtract the answer
from 5. Add the ten results and multiply by 2.5, which gives a score out of
100. 68 is the commonly cited average. `results.md` has the columns.
