# Your task

*Give this to the participant. Read it to them only if they ask.*

---

**Imagine you've just finished school (or you're studying) and want to work
out which job to aim for, and how to get there.**

**Use this website until it shows you a plan to reach a job.**

Tell me when you think you're done. There's no time limit, and there are no
wrong answers.

---

## Afterwards: three questions

1. In your own words, what did the plan at the end tell you?
2. Was there anything you didn't understand, or a point where you weren't sure
   what to do next?
3. Would you use this to plan your own next step? Why, or why not?

## Afterwards: ten statements (System Usability Scale)

For each one, circle how much you agree:
1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree.
