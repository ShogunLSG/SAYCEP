# Taking part: information and consent

**Project:** SAYCEP, a career-planning prototype built for the ISJ107V module.
**Researcher:** Lesego Matsimela, a student, working alone on this project.

## What you'd do

You'll use a website for about 10 minutes, then answer a few short questions.
**We're testing the website, not you.** If something is confusing, that's
the website's fault, and it's exactly what I need to find out.

## What I record

- whether you got to the end of the task, how long it took, and how many
  screens you went through;
- where you hesitated or got stuck, written down by me as you go;
- your answers to the questions.

I don't record your name, your face or your voice. You're "P1", "P2" and so
on. Any skills you tick are used only during the session. The results appear,
without names, in my assessed coursework and presentation.

## Your choices

- Taking part is **voluntary**.
- You can **stop at any time** without giving a reason. If you stop, your
  results won't be used.
- You can skip any question.
- You need to be **18 or older** to take part.
